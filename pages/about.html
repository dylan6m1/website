About Me

I’m a Bay Area–based photographer whose interest in film photography comes from both its technical demands and its ability to express mood. I enjoy working within the constraints of physics—light, exposure, and timing—to create images that feel deliberate and grounded.

My work often explores liminal spaces and quiet environments, focusing on atmosphere over explanation. I’m always experimenting, allowing both intention and uncertainty to shape the final image.
